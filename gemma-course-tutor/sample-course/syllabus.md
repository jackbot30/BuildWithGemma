# Sample Course — Coordinate Geometry & Algebra Basics

> An original, freely redistributable mini-course bundled with this repo so the
> tutor and eval harness run out of the box. Not derived from any external course.

## Unit 1: Coordinate Geometry

- **1.1 The Coordinate Plane**
  - Axes, quadrants, and reading ordered pairs
- **1.2 Distance Between Two Points**
  - The distance formula and worked examples
- **1.3 Slope of a Line**
  - Rise over run and the slope formula

## What you can ask the tutor

Try questions grounded in these lessons, for example:

- "How do I find the distance between two points?"
- "What is the slope formula?"
- "Which quadrant is the point (-3, 4) in?"
