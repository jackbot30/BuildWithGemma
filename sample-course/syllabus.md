# Foundations of Algebra — Unit 3: Linear & Quadratic Functions

> This is a **self-written sample course** that ships in the repo so Math Sync has
> something to teach out of the box. The real (copyrighted) course is loaded locally
> and is never committed. Point the app at any folder shaped like this one.

## About this unit

A short unit covering the two function families a first-year algebra student meets
first: **linear** and **quadratic**. Every lesson ends with worked examples whose
final answers double as ground truth for Math Sync's answer checker.

## Lessons

1. [Linear Equations](lessons/01-linear-equations.md) — slope-intercept form, solving
   for a variable, graphing a line.
2. [Quadratic Functions](lessons/02-quadratics.md) — standard vs vertex form, the
   quadratic formula, finding roots and the vertex.

## What you should be able to do by the end

- Solve a linear equation for `x` and state the slope and intercept of a line.
- Solve a quadratic by the quadratic formula and identify its roots and vertex.
- Sketch either function and explain what each parameter does to the graph.

## How answers are checked

Every final numeric or algebraic answer the tutor gives is verified **deterministically**
against the lesson answer keys (or an independent computation) — not by asking the model
whether it was right. Word problems and "show your steps" reasoning are taught but not
machine-verified; the ✓/✗ badge covers final computable answers only.
